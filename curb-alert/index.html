Curb Alert

A working prototype of a garage-sale finder, sized for a phone. Everything below is live: filter the listings, post one of your own, tune your account.

Sales you post are saved to this artifact's shared store, so they show up in Find for anyone who opens this page — on a phone too.

Find
Distance, day, sale type, categories, payment, access, and a search that reads item lists.
List
Five steps, with an optional item list you can price out and export.
Account
Profile, saved sales, your listings, saved searches, alerts, privacy.
